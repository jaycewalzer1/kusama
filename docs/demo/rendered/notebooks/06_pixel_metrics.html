06 — pixel measurement over the corpus, including one real null¶

Summary. This notebook does not recompute any pixel metric. corpus surface, corpus vocabulary and corpus audit have careful definitions — what a sheet's ground is, what counts as an untouched margin, how a permutation baseline is drawn — and a PIL re-implementation would be a second thing to keep correct. So this prints the CLI's own output and adds only the pictures.

The findings the CLI reports, and they are mostly negative:

  • Only 9 of 1,500 sampled images are sheets with a measurable ground. Museums shoot 2D works with the mount inside the frame, so plate-unit measurement mostly fails on this corpus.
  • Real sheets touch their edges (edgeContact median 0.06–0.13) while all 7 measurable plates read 0.0000 top, right and left. The artist's plates and the corpus's sheets differ in a way the envelope's 0..1 binning cannot express.
  • The reading-vs-pixel audit is a REAL NULL: off-centre median 0.0439 against centred 0.0440, chance 100%, over a 40x offset range. That is not an instrument failure — the instrument has the range and there is nothing there.

Aspect, the one metric that is safe to read from the manifest¶

Width and height are recorded by the ingest, not inferred, so this is the one geometric fact that does not depend on a segmentation working.

a = kept.aspect.dropna()
print(f"corpus aspect: median {a.median():.3f}, IQR {a.quantile(.25):.3f}..{a.quantile(.75):.3f}")
for d in k.trajectory_dirs():
    t = k.load_trajectory(d)
    pa = pd.Series([p["aspect"] for p in t["plates"]])
    pct = (a < pa.median()).mean()

          f"{pct:.1%} of corpus images are narrower")
