# ur-stencil

**Target.** Underground Resistance lineage: black and steel plus one red, stencil-cut display type, text as coordinates and orders, no faces, no polish.

**Tried.** Stencil bridges are three ground-coloured `solid` bars laid *across* the silver word TRANSMIT by a `repeat` — the cut is drawn on rather than taken out, since there is no mask primitive. Misregistration is real, not simulated: OR REFUSE is printed twice, a red text op at (405.5, 425) and a steel one at (400, 420). Chevrons are hand-authored six-point polygons in a 7x2 grid with a second `repeat` punching red slots through the lower row. The orders block is `solid` steel with black type reversed out of it. No fragment is placed anywhere on the sheet, so "no faces" is structural rather than stylistic.

**Could not do.** Nothing here is actually cut: a bridge is a bar of ground colour, so it works only over a flat ground and would fail immediately over a photograph or a texture. Coordinates had to be split into one text op per line because `text` does not honour `\n`. The display face is PT Sans Regular — no bold, no condensed, no stencil face — so the word reads as *tracked-out sans* rather than as a stencil, and the whole stencil claim rests on the three drawn-on bridges.
