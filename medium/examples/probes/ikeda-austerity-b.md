# ikeda-austerity-b

**Target.** The same target as `ikeda-austerity`, inverted: a white field, black hairlines, one dense black data slab, one blue interruption.

**Tried.** The whole sheet is reversed out of the first variant, to test whether the substrate's competence is the black ground or the hard-edged geometry. It is the geometry — nothing in this program is any harder to draw on white than on black, because `solid` and `text` carry their own colour and never consult the ground. The comb is 96 one-pixel black rects; the slab is a black rect with 132 paper-coloured rects scattered back through it, which is again the only element on the sheet the seed decides. 571 marks, cost 187: the cheapest of the twelve probes and the most convincing.

**Could not do.** Still no monospace, so the tabular readout is PT Sans Regular letter-spaced with `tracking` and its columns do not truly align. Still no rule finer than one device pixel, and no rule *softer* than one either — `rule` is a brush line with grain, so every hairline here is a `solid` rect of `h: 1`.
