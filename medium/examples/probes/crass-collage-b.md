# crass-collage-b

**Target.** The same target as `crass-collage`, inverted: a montage printed as a negative — white figures on a black field, with the type in the only other colour.

**Tried.** Inverting the plate turns the pack's fifteen vector outlines from bad photographs into good stencils. A silhouette that reads as a blob in black on white reads as a cut-out in white on black, because the eye stops asking it for detail and starts reading it as a shape. This is the only move in the whole test that improves what the fragment library can do without adding anything to it. The architecture is `arch.arch` as a rotring outline over a hatched sky; `light.beam` in blood is the one saturated mass; the crowd is a `repeat` of six `figure.crowd` at span 210 so the heads stay small enough to read as a crowd rather than as figures.

**Could not do.** Still no photographic material and still no continuous tone — those are the two hard impossibles and inverting the plate does not touch either. The hatch is engraving at a fixed spacing with no tonal ramp, there is no gradient style, and no way to modulate a fill across a region, so every value on this sheet is exactly one of the four palette colours. The type is not knocked out of the image either; it is bone-coloured type on a blood bar drawn over the top.
